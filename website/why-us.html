Your teachers are working freelancers and online earners themselves. They teach the exact workflows they use with real clients — so what you learn is what the market pays for today, not last year’s theory.

100% practical classes

Every session is hands-on. You edit the video, build the page, write the proposal — in class, with the teacher beside you. You leave each class having built something real.

Learning the skill is only half the journey. We help you set up your Fiverr and Upwork profiles, review your gigs, and polish your proposals until your first order lands.

Fair fees for Shujabad families, with easy installment plans. Money should never be the reason talent stays at home.

Alumni groups, job referrals, and collaboration with students from the batches before and after you. At iSkillSpot you are never learning alone.
