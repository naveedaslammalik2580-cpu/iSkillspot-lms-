About iSkillSpot

Learning together, growing together

iSkillSpot started in Shujabad in 2023 with a simple goal: help young people learn useful digital skills close to home.

We believe talent is already here. Students only need the right place, the right guidance, and a chance to grow.

At iSkillSpot, learning is practical. Students do real work, build confidence, and develop skills they can use for freelancing, jobs, and online earning.

Our aim is not only to teach courses. We want to help students move forward with confidence, purpose, and better opportunities.

Meet the Founder

A message from our founder

“I believe every young person has something special to offer. Many students have talent and interest, but they do not always know where to begin.

That is why iSkillSpot was created as a place where students feel welcome, learn useful skills, and get the support they need.

Our goal is simple: when a student joins iSkillSpot, they should leave with more knowledge, more confidence, and a clearer direction for their future.”

Amir Nazir — Founder, iSkillSpot
